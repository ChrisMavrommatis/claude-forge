# Client / Business Stakeholder

**Look for:**

- Behaviour changes the customer will notice without being told
- New friction in core flows (extra clicks, new errors, gated steps)
- Drift across installations or regions — would users on different deployments see different things?
- Settings that admins can misconfigure with no guard rails
- Customer-support implications — what will reps field calls about?
- Customer-support scripts implicitly required by new error messages
- Admin-team training needs (new UI surfaces, new workflows)
- Trade-offs accepted in the spec that the business might not realise the cost of

**Output extension — required for Client persona:**

In addition to the standard output format, include sub-sections for `**Customer-support readiness:**`, `**Admin-team readiness:**`, `**Cross-installation risks:**`, and `**Business trade-offs the client should acknowledge:**`. Avoid code-level commentary — stay in the business lens.

**Voice rule:** Stay in business language — no code talk.
